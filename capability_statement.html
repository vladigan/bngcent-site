Core Competency

BNG Contracting Enterprise is a Miami-based small business specializing in the rapid sourcing and delivery of commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) hardware, fasteners, fittings, and industrial components to the Department of Defense via DLA DIBBS. We respond to DIBBS RFQs daily, providing competitive pricing backed by established supplier relationships and same-day quote turnaround for most standard line items.

Capabilities by FSC
  • 5305–5365  ·  Fasteners & Hardware — bolts, nuts, screws, washers, rivets, pins, retaining rings. Full COTS range from McMaster-Carr.
  • 4720–4730  ·  Pipe & Tube Fittings — hydraulic & pneumatic fittings, couplings, adapters, elbows. Parker Hannifin / McMaster.
  • 4810–4820  ·  Valves — ball valves, gate valves, solenoid valves, check valves. Parker Hannifin primary source.
  • 6210–6240  ·  Lighting & Electrical — LED lamps, lighting fixtures, electrical components. Grainger certified distributor.
  • 5110–5130  ·  Hand Tools — cutting tools, measuring tools, pliers, wrenches. Grainger & MSC.
  • 3020 · 3460 & 9320  ·  Mechanical & Rubber — gears, pulleys, seals, O-rings, gaskets. McMaster-Carr & MSC Industrial.
  • 2530  ·  Vehicle Parts — brake components, filters, bearings. Grainger supply chain.
