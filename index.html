How We Operate

From Solicitation to Awarded Contract

Three stages. Running around the clock. Contracts won, not just submitted.

01
Opportunity Discovery

We monitor every DIBBS solicitation and SAM.gov posting in real time. Each opportunity is scored for margin potential, competition density, and fulfillment viability — only winnable contracts reach our pipeline.

02
Precision Bid Pricing

We cross-reference government estimated values, FPDS award history, and live distributor pricing across 6 supplier networks. Every bid is priced to win at your target margin with supplier fulfillment confirmed before submission.

03
Contract Award

Bids are submitted directly to DIBBS with zero manual entry and no missed deadlines. Every submission is logged, tracked, and followed through to award — then we manage fulfillment coordination with your suppliers.
